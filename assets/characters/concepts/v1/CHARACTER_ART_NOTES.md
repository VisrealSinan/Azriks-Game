# Azriks Game: first character concepts

Status: concept art for review, not game-ready sprites or animation atlases.

## Files
- `visreal-sinan-concept-v1.png`: Visreal Sinan as a taller street explorer, carrying an amber lantern and wearing a practical long travel coat.
- `azrik-concept-v1.png`: Azrik as the compact winged companion in his established black-and-red jacket.

Both images are 1536 × 1024 RGB concept boards with a main pose, head detail, front view and back view. Backgrounds are intentionally opaque. View placement and proportions are illustrative, not measured rigging specifications.

## Identity and proposed changes
Azrik retains his saved avatar's short black hair, red eyes, pointed crimson ears, crimson feathered wings and black clothing with red trim. The ears are not horns, and the wings are not bat wings.

Visreal draws on the previously supplied Sin character references. The long coat, satchel and lantern are proposed exploration gear. The taller scale distinguishes him from Azrik. These choices remain open for review and do not establish a real-world likeness.
